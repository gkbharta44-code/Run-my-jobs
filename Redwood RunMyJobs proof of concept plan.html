Redwood RunMyJobs proof of concept

Six-week plan to test RunMyJobs on the Track A order-to-settlement flow, with questions for Redwood and a week 6 go/no-go checklist.

1. Six-week timeline

Week 6 ends with the go/no-go decision, which depends on the Delper investment terms being confirmed.

3. Questions for Redwood

  1. Sandbox: Can you provide a trial environment within the first week, and who is the named engineer for the proof of concept?
  2. SAP integration: Which SAP versions and connectors do you support, and how does RunMyJobs integrate with SAP BTP and Signavio?
  3. Agent governance: How do your MCP server and A2A plug-in enforce role-based access, audit trails and human approval before a state-changing action? Can you demonstrate this on our Track A flow?
  4. Pricing: What is the licence quote for our expected volume? What renewal price cap can you commit to in writing?
  5. Exit and data: What are the data export formats and timelines on exit? What happens to our workflow definitions if we leave?
  6. Disaster recovery: What are your recovery time and recovery point objectives under the single-tenant SaaS option?
  7. Mainframe: Since the summary includes industrial and legacy systems, what is your approach for on-premises job execution through a gateway?
